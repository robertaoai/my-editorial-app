## 5. Surgical inversion-thinking cross-reference (supersedes prior version)

**Target:** Addendum v1.4 (Three Lines Model aligned). **Method:** for each discipline drawn from surgical safety-case governance, invert it (what failure appears if this control is removed) and check whether the current Addendum already covers that failure mode.

**Uncertainty flag (cupcake):** this section replaces the earlier "Surgical Architect concept" table from Entry 002, which depended on an unconfirmed source document (see Section 9). This version does not. It builds inversion heuristics from a general safety-case pattern (fail-safe defaults, plan locks, irreversibility limits) common to regulated life-critical domains, not from any single named framework. It does not claim IEC 62304, ISO 14971, or ASTM F2491 as sources; those are named only to be clear they were not researched here. Every row is a proposed gap, not a decided fact. Nothing here is authorized for build until the Chief Editor accepts a row into the Charter or a future addendum via the Ratification Log.

| # | Surgical discipline | Editorial gap if inverted | Current Addendum v1.4 coverage | Downgrade rule (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Fail-safe default: uncertainty halts and escalates, doesn't proceed | No confidence floor. `reviewer_confidence` (Low/Medium/High) exists at T3, but a Low-confidence agent can still complete its gate and pass the article forward. | Missing-data handling exists but no halt trigger. | Low confidence at T2/T3 forces a mandatory Line 2 flag at T5 entry, not an auto-stall. Medium proceeds flagged; High proceeds normally. |
| S2 | Plan lock: the plan is frozen once set; drift requires re-approval | `editorial_angle` (T3) and `meaning_invariance_checklist` (T4) exist, but nothing detects if the angle drifts between them. | Checklist checks completion, not consistency. | Angle change during drafting logs a `plan_deviation` flag visible to Line 2 at T5. Doesn't auto-revert to T3; Line 2 can trigger T8 if the drift is material. |
| S3 | Irreversibility prevention: block harm before it's unrecoverable | T8 returns Published articles to revision, but the live WordPress/LinkedIn post isn't actively removed. No `Retracted` state exists. | Recall exists; depublication doesn't. | T8 recall from Published triggers a `Depublication` action (WordPress to draft, LinkedIn deleted) and sets a new terminal state, `Retracted`, distinct from `Needs Revision`. |
| S4 | Adverse-event taxonomy: deviations are severity-classified, not logged flat | `EmergencyBypass`/`HumanOverride` are logged as event types, but a near-miss and an actual bad publish carry equal audit weight. | Event types exist; severity doesn't. | Add `event_severity` (NearMiss/Minor/Major/Sentinel) to the audit log. Sentinel events trigger mandatory Line 3 review and a Chief Editor alert. |
| S5 | Redundancy: no single point of failure on the critical path | Chief Editor is the only Line 2 executor. Addendum §6.4 names this risk explicitly but specifies no mitigation. | Risk acknowledged; no backup defined. | Define a Deputy Editor (Line 2, limited authority: can run T5 review and T11 publish confirmation, can't approve new topics or trigger emergency bypass). |
| S6 | Risk stratification: assessed before work begins, not after | Risk signals exist but fire at T5. High-risk articles pass through T1 to T4 before any risk-based scrutiny. | Reactive, not upfront. | Compute `risk_stratification` at T1 from source tier, topic novelty, and engagement anomaly. High-risk articles get a mandatory Line 2 checkpoint at T3, not just T5. |
| S7 | Graceful degradation: reduced function, not total failure, when a part fails | If the Chief Editor is unavailable, every article needing T5 stalls; there's no reduced-capacity mode. | Stall behavior documented (§6.4); no degraded mode. | Define `degraded_mode`: Line 1 agents keep running T1 to T4 while Line 2 is out. Articles queue at T5. No publishing occurs until Line 2 returns. |
| S8 | Positive proof of safety: prove it's safe, don't just fail to find a problem | T5/T6 checks are all "no problem found," not a positive readiness attestation. | Absence-based, not presence-based. | Add `publication_readiness_attestation` (boolean, non-empty text, actor ID, timestamp) as required T6 fields. Shifts the default from proceed-unless-blocked to proceed-because-confirmed. |
| S9 | Mandatory post-action review: every case reviewed, not just failures | Line 3 review is risk-triggered only. Articles that publish cleanly get no review at all. | Conditional, not systematic. | Define `post_publication_sample_review`: Line 3 reviews a random 5 to 10 percent of published articles weekly, non-blocking, feeding calibration. |

S3, S5, S9 mainly change what Line 2 and Line 3 are responsible for (depublication authority, backup coverage, sample audits).
